# Audio and sync

In chart information, MiaCode can set or detect the BPM and offset.

After creating a chart, open chart information and enter a known BPM manually, or click **Auto detect**. MiaCode fills in the result automatically.

Click **Auto detect offset** on the chart information page to fill in the detected result.

Supported audio extensions include `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.flac`, and `.ogg`.
